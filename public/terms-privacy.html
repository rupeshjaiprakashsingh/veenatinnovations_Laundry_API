Liability for Garments (Laundry Service)

Grivana exercises utmost care in handling all garments entrusted to us. However, due to the inherent risks associated with fabric care, our liability is limited as follows:

1. Scope of Liability

Grivana shall not be held responsible for:

  • Pre-existing damage, wear and tear, or inherent fabric defects (including shrinkage, colour bleeding, or loose embellishments).
  • Items without care labels or those requiring special handling not disclosed at the time of pickup.
  • Ornaments, buttons, sequins, or accessories attached to garments.

2. Compensation Limits

In the event of proven damage or loss caused during our ironing process, Grivana's liability shall be limited to a maximum of three times the ironing charge of the garment, or ₹1,000 per garment, whichever is lower. Compensation will be provided in the form of service credit, not cash.

3. Claim Window

Customers must report any damage or loss within 48 hours of delivery. Claims made beyond this period will not be entertained.

4. Customer Responsibilities

Customers are required to:

  • Check pockets and remove valuables before handing over garments.
  • Declare delicate fabrics or special care requirements at pickup.
  • Verify garment count at the time of delivery.

5. Optional Insurance

For premium garments, Grivana offers optional garment insurance at an additional charge. This provides extended coverage up to ₹3,000 per garment.
