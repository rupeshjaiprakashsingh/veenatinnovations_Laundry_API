Privacy Policy

Operated by SaiMorphix Innovations LLP

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to provide, including your name, mobile number, email address, locality, and any messages you send to us. When using our services, we may also collect booking details, pickup and delivery information, and garmentcare preferences necessary to provide the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide customer support
  • Assess service availability in your area
  • Arrange pickups and deliveries
  • Improve our services and app functionality
  • Send relevant service updates and notifications

We process your information only where there is a legitimate servicerelated reason to do so.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service partners who help us operate Grivana (such as delivery staff, technology providers, or customer support teams). These partners are required to handle the information securely and only for the agreed purpose.

4. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal information. We retain data only as long as necessary to provide services, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You may request to review, update, or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable legal requirements. You may also opt out of nonessential marketing communications at any time by contacting us.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services evolve. The latest version will always be available on this page.
